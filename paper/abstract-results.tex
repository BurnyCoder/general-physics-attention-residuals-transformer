We test Attention Residuals in the General Physics Transformer (GPhyT), reusing the released GPhyT backbone and Flash Linear Attention reference operator. Standard and Block variants are assigned six paired seeds, with three-seed Full and uniform-routing controls. The selected convection and radiative-flow datasets use official trajectory splits, training-only normalization, and $128\times64$ resolution. The primary endpoint compares the equally weighted dataset log ratios of twelve-step autoregressive NMSE at a fixed optimizer-update count. The total project limit is 24 GPU-hours. At this revision, 0/18 planned training runs are complete. Complete finite endpoints for all six primary pairs are not established, so the primary effect is undefined. Implementation checks and prospective power do not establish an accuracy or stability improvement.
