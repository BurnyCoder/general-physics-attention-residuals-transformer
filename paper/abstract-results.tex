We compare GPhyT with Block Attention Residuals against GPhyT without Attention Residuals, reusing the released GPhyT backbone and Flash Linear Attention reference operator. Both models train from scratch with six paired seeds, identical shared-parameter initialization and sampled training examples, the same optimizer schedule, effective batch size 16, and 3,175 updates. Standard GPhyT retains its original additive residuals and transformer self-attention. Three-seed Full and uniform-routing variants provide exploratory controls. The selected convection and radiative-flow datasets use official trajectory splits, training-only normalization, and $128\times64$ resolution. The primary endpoint compares equally weighted dataset log ratios of twelve-step autoregressive NMSE. The total project limit is 24 GPU-hours. At this revision, 18/18 planned training runs are complete. Relative to GPhyT without Attention Residuals, Block reduces the primary error endpoint by 24.52\% (95\% CI [11.56, 35.58]\%; exact paired $p=0.03125$). The interval supports the prespecified minimum 5\% improvement. Claims are restricted to this model scale, selected data, resolution, and training budget.
